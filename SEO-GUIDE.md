## 🚀 ACTION STEPS (Do These Now):

### Step 1: Submit to Google Search Console (MOST IMPORTANT!)

**Time: 10 minutes**

1. Go to: https://search.google.com/search-console
2. Click "Add Property"
3. Enter: `https://vtuvault.online`
4. Choose "DNS verification" method
5. Google will give you a TXT record like:
   ```
   google-site-verification=abc123xyz456
   ```
6. Add this to Hostinger DNS:
   - Type: TXT
   - Name: @
   - Value: (paste the code from Google)
7. Click "Verify" in Google Search Console
8. Submit sitemap: `https://vtuvault.online/sitemap.xml`

**Expected Result:** Google will start crawling your site within 24-48 hours
